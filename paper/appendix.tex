\section{Detailed Protocol and Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

\subsection{Frozen experimental record}

The manuscript is produced from a completed and frozen experimental record; no experiments were rerun for paper preparation. The final code state is commit \texttt{ceb6122}, and the official H6 protocol was frozen at commit \texttt{3cbd3c5}. Repository validation records confirm 30/30 paired H6 JSON/NPZ traces, 18/18 related tests, 53/53 immutable historical scientific artifacts, and 117/117 SHA-256 manifest checks. Commit hashes are provided without an identifying repository URL to preserve anonymity.

The authoritative machine-readable summaries are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item controlled local Push-T diagnosis and repair summaries;
  \item Wall diagnosis and repair summaries, with separate development, diagnosis, and repair-state manifests; and
  \item the official H6 aggregate summary plus all 30 paired per-episode raw traces.
\end{itemize}
Each headline value in the paper is mapped to its report section and saved artifact in the anonymized supplementary evidence audit.

\subsection{Candidate sets and intervention controls}

In controlled local Push-T, every condition uses the same 81-action reference family and the same 65-query budget per anchor. In Wall, all readout comparisons within a diagnosis state use the same one-dimensional waypoint candidates. For fixed-trace H6 attribution, all scores rank the identical 9,001 sequences retained from the baseline trace. Consequently, each fixed-trace regret contrast measures selection on matched candidates rather than proposal quality.

The adaptive H6 comparison is deliberately labeled differently. Baseline and readout CEM share horizon, population, elite count, query budget, initialization, and random-seed policy, but their candidate traces diverge once scores change the elite set. The trace union is a finite diagnostic reference, not an oracle over the continuous action space.

\subsection{Statistical unit and confidence intervals}

Local Push-T and Wall bootstrap independent anchor states; H6 bootstraps episodes. Candidate actions and repeated queries within a state are dependent and are not resampled as independent trials. All reported confidence intervals are paired 95\% nonparametric bootstrap intervals from the frozen analyses. The repair accounting retains every improved, unchanged, and harmed state or episode.

\section{Claim Boundaries}

The evidence does not support a universal claim that latent planners are prediction limited or metric limited. It does not make oracle gaps additive or uniquely causal, and the FP32 control does not test a general predictor improvement. The official H6 scorer repair is not statistically significant under the frozen criterion. The controlled local Push-T protocol is not the official planner. Finally, the experiments do not establish transfer to real robots, learned policies, or vision--language--action systems.
