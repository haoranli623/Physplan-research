\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Planning through a latent world model is a multi-interface decision pipeline. Controlled oracle substitutions identify decision-metric headroom in a local Push-T action family and prediction headroom in a disjoint-state Wall validation; pre-specified repairs improve the diagnosed interfaces in those controlled regimes. On the official Push-T H6 CEM trace, both gaps are positive, the prediction point estimate is larger under the frozen rule, and rescoring changes search coverage; the scorer stress test is directionally positive but inconclusive. The supported conclusion is methodological and bounded: recoverable-headroom ordering can change across planning protocols. Diagnose before retraining the world model or changing the planner.
