\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Planning through a latent world model is a multi-interface decision pipeline. Controlled oracle substitutions identify decision-metric headroom in a local Push-T action family and prediction headroom in an independent Wall replication; pre-specified repairs recover the diagnosed gaps in both controlled regimes. Under the official Push-T H6 CEM planner, prediction becomes the larger limitation and rescoring changes search coverage, so the scorer intervention is directionally positive but inconclusive. The supported conclusion is methodological and bounded: the dominant recoverable bottleneck can migrate with the planning regime. Diagnose before retraining the world model or changing the planner.
