\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models for control.}
Learned dynamics models support planning from pixels or compact state representations, from PlaNet and probabilistic ensemble control to recent scalable latent-control systems \citep{hafner2019planet,chua2018pets,hansen2024tdmpc2}. DINO-WM plans over frozen visual features without reconstructing observations \citep{zhou2025dinowm}; V-JEPA~2 and JEPA-WMs study action-conditioned joint-embedding prediction for physical planning \citep{assran2025vjepa2,terver2026jepawms}. These works establish strong model-and-planner pipelines. We instead hold a pipeline fixed and ask which interface offers recoverable decision headroom.

\paragraph{Prediction quality and decision utility.}
Objective-mismatch studies show that forward prediction error need not track control quality \citep{lambert2020objective}, while planning-aware metrics show that prediction errors of similar generic magnitude can have different downstream consequences \citep{ivanovic2021planningaware}. Value-aware loss functions, value-targeted regression, and value-equivalent models likewise formalize that models should preserve information relevant to decisions rather than optimize generic one-step prediction alone \citep{farahmand2017valueaware,ayoub2020vtr,grimm2020valueequivalence,voelcker2025calibrated}. PhysPlan does not claim this premise or task-aligned scoring as new. It addresses the operational question that precedes method choice: for a frozen planner, which interface has recoverable decision headroom?

\paragraph{Oracle diagnostics and our distinction.}
Replacing a learned component with ground truth is a standard diagnostic instrument. IMWM shows that finite-budget search can remain limiting even with idealized environment dynamics, then uses an intuition model to guide latent planning \citep{gao2026imwm}; VIScore diagnoses planning-relevant world-model quality across encoder, predictor, and planner properties \citep{wu2026viscore}. PhysPlan does not claim the general observation that prediction is not the only bottleneck. Its distinction is candidate-matched, interface-level recoverable-headroom attribution across prediction, metric, proposal, and within-search selection, followed by prospective diagnosis-to-repair tests where available. Conditions share candidate actions and simulator evaluation, and the independent unit is the initial state rather than individual candidates. This supports localization under explicit substitutions, not a unique causal decomposition; Wall further separates diagnosis, repair training, and evaluation by disjoint state sets.

\paragraph{Sampling-based planning.}
CEM iteratively samples candidates, selects elites, and updates its proposal \citep{rubinstein1999cem}; this pattern is common in model-based control \citep{chua2018pets,hafner2019planet}. A planner may fail because its queries never cover a good action or because it misranks actions it did query. Retaining every query and simulator-evaluating those candidates separates coverage from within-trace selection. It also exposes an important limit of fixed-trace attribution: once a scorer changes CEM elites, it changes the future proposal distribution as well as ranking.
